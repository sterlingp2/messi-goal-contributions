Sources, definitions and what is missing
Sources
Transfermarkt: every assist outside StatsBomb's tracking (Champions League, cups, Argentina) with scorer, minute and assist type, taken from match reports, and per-match assist counts for every season. Transfermarkt's goal list: the minute of each Argentina friendly goal. Wikipedia: final score, score at the time, venue and penalty flag for every Argentina goal, and the minute of every competitive Argentina goal (from the tournament match records). StatsBomb open data (every La Liga match Messi played 2004/05 to 2020/21, plus the 2009, 2011 and 2015 Champions League finals and the 2018 World Cup): exact shot and pass coordinates, minute, body part, xG and assists. Club goal log derived from Transfermarkt (all official Barcelona goals with minute, final score and assister). A hand-transcribed log of Messi's goals from video (shot location and body part for every goal, including Argentina).
Score and H/A
Final score is always Messi's team first. H/A is home or away; N is a neutral venue where the source says so.
Assist
Transfermarkt's definition, used everywhere so totals match public figures. It counts a pass, cross or set piece that led directly to a goal, a rebound from a Messi shot (assist type "Shot on goal"), and a penalty Messi won but a teammate scored ("Won penalty"). Every match's assist count agrees with Transfermarkt. In La Liga, where StatsBomb is stricter, the extra assists were located in StatsBomb's event data or settled from the match report.
Location
The pitch is drawn as the attacking half, goal at the top. StatsBomb locations come from tracked event data. Video locations are hand-placed and match StatsBomb to within about one percent of the pitch where both exist.
Not included yet
Pitch locations for assists outside La Liga, since no tracking data exists for those matches. Club friendlies such as the Joan Gamper Trophy, which are not official matches. Youth matches (2005 U-20 World Cup, 2008 Olympics), which are not senior caps.
Corrections made while merging
Argentina match dates follow Wikipedia's local date; the video log often had the next day's date. Penalty goals carry no assister. Teammate and opponent names are unified across sources. A penalty Messi won and scored himself is not an assist.
Data: StatsBomb open data, Transfermarkt, Wikipedia, and public goal logs. See the repository README for sources and method. Not affiliated with StatsBomb, Transfermarkt or FC Barcelona.
